# Orientation and control scheme by genre

Defaults, not rules. Check the game's actual verbs before applying a row.

| Genre | Orientation | Left thumb | Right thumb | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Racing, driving, riding (cars, bikes, scooters, boards) | Landscape | Analog stick: steer + throttle/brake, or steer only | Accelerate/brake pedals if not on the stick; boost, drift, stunts as icon buttons around one large primary | Offer "tilt to steer" and "buttons instead of stick" as settings. Auto-accelerate is a common accessibility option. |
| Third-person action, open world | Landscape | Floating move stick | Camera drag on the right half; attack, jump, dodge cluster | Camera drag and buttons share the right half; give buttons priority over drags that start on them. |
| Shooter (FPS/TPS) | Landscape | Floating move stick | Aim by dragging the right half; fire button, also auto-fire option | Separate look sensitivity setting. |
| Twin-stick shooter | Landscape | Move stick | Aim/fire stick | Both fixed sticks; fire while the aim stick is deflected. |
| Platformer | Landscape | Left/right (stick or two large buttons) | Jump (large), secondary action | Precise platformers often prefer digital left/right over an analog stick. |
| Fighting | Landscape | Directional pad or stick | 4–6 attack buttons in a fan | Offer simplified one-button specials. |
| Sports (football, basketball) | Landscape | Move stick | Pass, shoot, sprint; contextual labels by possession | Context-sensitive icons reduce button count. |
| MOBA | Landscape | Move stick | Ability buttons in an arc, drag-to-aim on each | Ability buttons double as aim sticks while held. |
| Endless runner | Portrait | Swipe left/right/up/down anywhere | Same surface | No on-screen controls needed. |
| Vertical shooter, bullet hell | Portrait | Relative drag anywhere moves the ship | Auto-fire | Ship offset above the finger so it is not covered. |
| Puzzle, match-3, card, board | Portrait | Tap and drag on items | Same | Keep actions within the lower two thirds for one-handed reach. |
| Idle, clicker, management | Portrait | Taps on UI | Same | Bottom navigation bar. |
| Tower defense, strategy | Either; landscape for wide maps | Pan by drag, pinch zoom | Build/ability bar along an edge | Avoid controls that cover the path. |

## Deciding when unsure

- Does the player steer or aim continuously while also pressing actions? Landscape, two thumbs.
- Can the game be played with one thumb? Portrait.
- Is the world wider than tall (roads, arenas, side-scrolling)? Landscape.
- Does the desktop version already exist with a 16:9 camera? Landscape keeps the same framing and HUD logic.

## Conventions worth keeping

- Movement and steering under the left thumb; actions under the right. Mirror only as a setting.
- The primary action is the largest button, nearest the right thumb's resting point (lower right).
- Secondary actions fan in an arc around the primary, within reach without moving the hand.
- Pause and menus at the top, away from accidental taps.
- Utilities used rarely (camera, reset, map) small and at the top.
